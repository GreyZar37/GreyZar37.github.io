Code-Driven Creativity

My Name is Oleg Sechovcov

I am a game developer with 4 years of industry experience, specializing in programming while also contributing across multiple disciplines, including game design, level design, 2D/3D art. I am currently taking bechlor in game devolepement and learning technology at the University of Southern Denmark (SDU). From 2022 to 2023, I expanded my expertise by teaching game development to school students at UngAalborg. In 2024, I joined Teacup Games, an indie game studio, where I continue to apply my skills to deliver engaging and innovative gaming experiences. In my spare time, I focus on developing horror game titles, a genre where my creativity and technical abilities truly shine. I enjoy joining gamejams, where I can collaborate with other developers and push the boundaries of my skills. I want to expand my network and connect with like-minded people in the industry.

My knowledge and skills

Programming

Proficient in writing clean, maintainable, and modular code. Strong understanding of object oriented programming and design patterns commonly used in game devolepement. Experienced in debugging complex gameplay systems and optimizing code for performance and memory usage. Developed core gameplay mechanics including combat systems, inventory management, quest system and dialogue systems. Understanding of third party APIs such as Steamworks and Mirror.

Game Design

Proficient in designing engaging core gameplay loops that effectively balance challenge and reward, ensuring satisfying moment-to-moment interactions. Skilled in creating compelling progression systems that foster long-term player engagement, including skill trees, unlockable content, and well-balanced in-game economies. Experienced in developing psychological horror mechanics that build sustained tension and evoke fear without relying on cheap jump scares. Strong understanding of horror design theory and its practical application, incorporating concepts such as fear of the unknown, isolation, environmental storytelling, and loss of player control to create immersive and unsettling experiences.

3D modelling

Proficient in creating low-poly 3D models that authentically replicate the visual aesthetic of the original old PlayStation games. Skilled at working within strict polygon count limits to optimize models for low-resolution rendering. Experienced in creating UV maps and textures that enhance the visual quality of low-poly models while maintaining a retro look. Creating game-ready assets, including characters, environments, and props, that are optimized for performance without sacrificing artistic integrity.

Story Writing

Proficient in crafting psychologically engaging horror narratives that focus on building tension, dread, and emotional unease rather than relying solely on shock value. Experienced in designing unsettling worlds and environments that act as narrative devices through environmental storytelling and symbolic visual cues Knowledgeable in leveraging horror theory concepts like Fear of the Unknown, Isolation, The Uncanny, and Loss of Control to create memorable and impactful narratives.Adept at collaborating with designers and audio teams to ensure that narrative beats are reinforced through visual and sound design, heightening emotional impact.

Unity

Proficient in using Unity for game development, including experience with the Unity Editor, scripting in C#, and implementing game mechanics. Knowledgeable in optimizing game performance and memory usage, as well as debugging and troubleshooting issues within the Unity environment. 4 years of experience in using Unity for game developement.

Project Management

Proficient in planning and managing game development projects from concept to release, ensuring milestones are met on time and within budget. Experienced with Agile and Scrum methodologies, including sprint planning, task prioritization, and regular stand-ups to keep teams aligned Familiar with version control and collaborative development workflows using Git, ensuring smooth integration of work across teams
